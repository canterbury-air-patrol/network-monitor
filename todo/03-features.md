# Phase 3: UAV Features & Ground Stations

> **Requires:** Phase 2 complete.

## Map & Real-time Display

> **Display priority:** RF coverage over the mission area is the primary display. UAV position is tracked by two external systems and is a secondary overlay. The coverage gap layer (Phase 10) should be on by default; UAV markers are optional and off by default. Design the map component with this hierarchy from the start.

- [x] **[P3-16]** Perform a "High-Glare" UI audit: verify all interactive elements meet WCAG AA contrast ratios and are operable with gloves (minimum 44 px touch targets). *(Referenced by: [P13-06])* *(`e2e/glare.ts` is the probe; `e2e/glare-audit.spec.ts` walks the states an operator can reach: the map and sidebar on load, the pin and edit forms, the station roster and its map popup, the expanded signal history, live and lost UAV markers, a crashed panel's fallback and the degraded-overlay notice. The probe measures what is rendered rather than a list of class names — it recovers every colour by painting it over black and again over white, so Tailwind's `color-mix()` alpha modifiers, inherited `opacity` and Recharts' SVG `fill` all resolve, and it composites the real backdrop stack before taking a ratio. Fixed: `text-white/50` and `/40` measured 4.4:1 and below — worse again over the `bg-white/15` rows — and became `text-slate-300`/`-400`; white on `bg-accent` was 3.7:1 and became `text-primary`; `text-red-300` on a highlighted roster row became `text-red-200`; the layers checkbox was a 20 px target and is now a 44 px row; Leaflet's 30 px zoom buttons and 24 px popup close button are padded out in `index.css`; and each map marker now centres its glyph in a 44 px `iconSize` box, which keeps the pin's precision while making it glove-sized. The sweep also caught the live UAV teardrop never loading at all: `Icon.Default` prepends a path it sniffs out of `leaflet.css` to whatever URL it is given, so Vite's hashed asset came back doubled and the marker drew its alt text instead — `imagePath = ''` settles it and the suite now asserts the icon decoded. [P13-06] adopts this spec as the CI accessibility check.)*
